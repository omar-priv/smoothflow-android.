## Scope and caveats

These are **curated imports, not complete clones** of every upstream repository. A skill whose `SKILL.md` refers to a script, dataset, hook, agent, or reference file that is not present here may need its full upstream package installed before those specific features can work. The upstream repositories remain the source of truth for updates, full assets, and licensing terms.

## Why these were selected

For document and printable-planner work, the most relevant guidance is information hierarchy, sensible grouping, readable Arabic typography, clear writing, contrast, and enough space to mark progress. The website-only parts of Taste Skill were not applied to this PDF.
